# Health and Care Worker visa

The Health and Care Worker visa is a dedicated route (a variant of the Skilled Worker route) for eligible medical professionals coming to work for the NHS, an NHS-supplier, or in adult social care.

## Who it's for

Doctors, nurses, health professionals, and adult social care workers in eligible occupation codes, sponsored by an approved employer such as the NHS, an organisation providing NHS services, or a Care Quality Commission-regulated adult social care provider.

## Key advantages over the standard Skilled Worker visa

- **Reduced fees**: a lower visa application fee than the standard Skilled Worker route.
- **Exempt from the Immigration Health Surcharge**: you and your dependants do not pay the IHS.
- **Fast-track processing**: typically faster decision times than standard work routes.
- **Lower salary thresholds** for many eligible occupations, reflecting NHS/social care pay scales.

## Core requirements

- A confirmed job offer from a licensed sponsor in an eligible health or social care occupation.
- The role must meet the relevant going rate/salary threshold for that occupation (generally lower than standard Skilled Worker thresholds).
- English language requirement at B1 level (or equivalent professional registration evidence in some cases).
- Relevant professional registration where required (e.g. General Medical Council, Nursing and Midwifery Council, Social Work England) depending on role.

## Visa length and settlement

Granted for up to 5 years at a time, and time on this route counts toward the 5 years continuous residence typically required for Indefinite Leave to Remain.

## Dependants

Partners and children can apply as dependants and are also exempt from the Immigration Health Surcharge.
