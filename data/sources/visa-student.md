# Student visa

The Student visa (formerly Tier 4) is for people aged 16 or over who have been offered a place on a course with a licensed student sponsor (a UK university, college, or school).

## Core requirements

- An unconditional offer of a place on a course from a licensed student sponsor, evidenced by a Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies (CAS).
- Sufficient funds to support yourself and pay course fees for the first year of study (or full course length if under 9 months), held for a 28-day period before applying.
- English language ability at the level required by your course and sponsor.
- Academic progression if you've previously studied in the UK on a student visa (your new course generally needs to be at a higher level).

## Visa length

Granted for the length of your course plus a short wrap-up period afterwards (typically 4 months for courses of 12 months or longer at degree level).

## Working while studying

Most full-time degree-level students at higher education providers can work up to 20 hours per week during term time and full-time during vacations, subject to sponsor confirmation. Restrictions are usually tighter for below-degree-level courses.

## Route after study

Graduates from UK universities on eligible courses can typically switch into the Graduate visa (a separate unsponsored route) after completing their degree, allowing 2 years of post-study work (3 years for PhD graduates) without needing a job offer.

## Dependants

Dependants are generally only permitted for postgraduate students on courses of 9 months or longer at research degree level, or those on government-sponsored study for 6 months or more — most taught postgraduate and undergraduate students cannot bring dependants.
