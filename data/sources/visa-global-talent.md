# Global Talent visa

The Global Talent visa is for leaders or potential leaders in academia, research, arts and culture, or digital technology who want to work in the UK. It does not require a job offer or a sponsoring employer.

## Who it's for

Split broadly into:
- **Academia or research**: fellows, researchers, or those endorsed via a recognised prize, or endorsed by UKRI, the Royal Society, the British Academy, or the Royal Academy of Engineering.
- **Arts and culture**: endorsed by Arts Council England covering combined arts, dance, literature, music, theatre, visual arts, and other creative fields.
- **Digital technology**: endorsed by Tech Nation's successor body / the relevant Home Office-approved endorsing body for exceptional talent or exceptional promise in areas like software engineering, product management, or data science.

## Two-stage process

1. **Endorsement**: apply to the relevant endorsing body for either "exceptional talent" (established leader) or "exceptional promise" (emerging leader) status.
2. **Visa application**: once endorsed, apply for the visa itself.

Some applicants with major recognised prizes (e.g. Nobel Prize, Fields Medal, Turing Award, BAFTA, Oscar) can skip the endorsement stage entirely.

## Key advantages

- No job offer or sponsor required — you can freelance, be self-employed, work for multiple employers, or set up a company.
- Faster route to settlement: as little as 3 years for exceptional talent endorsement holders (versus 5 years on most other work routes).
- No minimum salary requirement.

## Visa length and cost

Initial grant is typically up to 5 years, renewable. You pay the visa fee plus the Immigration Health Surcharge per year.

## Dependants

Partners and children can apply as dependants alongside or after the main applicant.
