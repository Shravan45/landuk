# Youth Mobility Scheme visa

The Youth Mobility Scheme (YMS) lets young people from participating countries and territories live and work in the UK for up to 2 years, without needing a job offer or sponsor.

## Who it's for

Applicants aged 18 to 30 (up to 35 for some nationalities) from participating countries/territories, which have historically included Australia, Canada, Japan, New Zealand, South Korea, Hong Kong, Taiwan, Andorra, Iceland, Monaco, San Marino, and others — the exact list and any annual quotas (ballots) vary by country, so always check current eligibility for your specific nationality.

## Core requirements

- Meet the age range for your nationality's scheme.
- Have £2,530 or more in savings (figure indicative — check current requirement).
- Not have children under 18 who live with you or for whom you're financially responsible.
- Some nationalities are selected via an annual ballot due to a capped number of places; others (e.g. Australia, Canada, New Zealand historically) are typically not capped.

## What you can do

- Work in most jobs (self-employment and setting up a business are also generally allowed, though company directorships have restrictions).
- Study, with some restrictions on courses requiring an ATAS certificate.
- Travel in and out of the UK freely during your visa period.

## Visa length

Up to 2 years, not extendable (some nationality-specific agreements allow slightly different lengths). This route does not lead directly to settlement — you'd need to switch to a different visa category such as Skilled Worker before your YMS visa expires if you want to stay longer.

## Dependants

You cannot bring dependants on this visa.
