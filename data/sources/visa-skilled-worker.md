# Skilled Worker visa

The Skilled Worker visa lets you come to or stay in the UK to do an eligible job with an employer that's been approved by the Home Office (a "licensed sponsor").

## Who it's for

You need a job offer from a UK employer holding a valid sponsor licence, in an eligible occupation on the going rate for that role. There is no longer a fixed shortage occupation list discount as of the points-based reforms — instead certain occupations on the Immigration Salary List get reduced going-rate thresholds.

## Core requirements (points-based)

You need 70 points total, made up of:
- 20 points: sponsorship by an approved employer for a job at the required skill level
- 20 points: job at the appropriate skill level (RQF level 3 or above, i.e. A-level equivalent or higher)
- 10 points: speaking English to the required standard (B1 on the Common European Framework)
- 20 points: salary at or above both the general salary threshold and the specific going rate for the occupation

## Salary threshold

The general salary threshold is reviewed periodically. As a rule of thumb you typically need to earn at least the higher of the general threshold or the specific going rate for your occupation code, with reduced thresholds for new entrants to the labour market, PhD holders in relevant subjects, and occupations on the Immigration Salary List.

## Visa length and cost

You can be granted up to 5 years at a time. Fees vary by visa length and whether the role is on the Immigration Salary List, and you must also pay the Immigration Health Surcharge (IHS) per year of stay in addition to the application fee.

## Route to settlement

Continuous lawful residence on the Skilled Worker route can lead to Indefinite Leave to Remain (settlement), typically after 5 years, provided absence limits and continuous employment conditions are met.

## Dependants

You can usually bring a partner and children as dependants if you can support them financially, subject to the same Immigration Health Surcharge per dependant.
